# Trọng Thưa Huỳnh's research group

Notes from his email of 15 October 2026, a reply to "Writing papers with
AI - want to try it with your group?". He leads a research group with
students and PhD students.

## What he said

- AI tools for writing papers are OK. But journals and conferences now
  check strictly for AI-written work. He is still willing to try.
- He asked for an **online session** where Nhat presents the tool to his
  group. Not answered yet (16 October 2026).

## Topics his group plans to work on

| Topic | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Energy-aware Edge AI | Use less energy when machine-learning models run on edge devices (small devices near the data, not in the cloud) |
| GNN in WSN | Graph neural networks used in wireless sensor networks |

The shared problem: optimize how machine-learning models are deployed on
edge devices.

## Data

- Mostly public data from GitHub and the internet.
- They plan a project to collect their own dataset for one specific
  problem.

## Where they publish

- IEEE, Springer, and ScienceDirect (Elsevier) journals and conferences.
- Minimum for students and PhD students: **Scopus-indexed**.
- Preferred: journals in **Q1 or Q2**. Q3 sometimes.

## What this means for the kit

- Strict AI checking at the venues is the reason to use this kit's gates
  (`make check`), not an autonomous pipeline on its own.
- Check `docs/venues.md` for IEEE, Springer and Elsevier AI disclosure
  rules before submission.
- On 16 October 2026 Nhat sent him the AutoResearchClaw repo link to
  forward to his students. See `docs/autoresearchclaw.md` for how ARC
  fits with the kit.
